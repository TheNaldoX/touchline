DataDivingGoalkeepers — Rafael Monteiro and collaborators (2022).

https://github.com/rafaellmmonteiro/DataDivingGoalkeepers
GPL-3.0. Modified positional data: landmark reconstruction, scale normalization, smoothing, extraction and retiming.
The hand-extension landmark is not a measured ball-contact timestamp.
Source trials and conversion script are supplied with the Touchline workspace. No endorsement by the researchers.
